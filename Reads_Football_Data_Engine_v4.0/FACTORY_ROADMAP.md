# Factory roadmap after v1.4

Next rule families, in order:
1. Verified cross-domain event sequences (CFB award → NFL draft, college → pro).
2. Arbitrary boolean nesting `(A AND B) OR (C AND NOT D)`.
3. Numeric comparisons and ranges (height, weight, stats once season totals pass QA).
4. Career-path sequence rules.
5. User-defined clue templates and distractor policies.
6. Telemetry-trained difficulty and automatic rule tuning.
